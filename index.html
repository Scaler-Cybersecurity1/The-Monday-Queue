    The Monday Queue

    You open your shift at Driftwood's SOC and the overnight queue is full: 12 alerts waiting. Most are noise. One host is genuinely compromised, and the signal is split across a few alerts. Your job is to triage the whole pile, then find and escalate the real intrusion.

    Your roleTier-1 SOC analyst
    Time needed25–35 minutes
    You willTriage 12 alerts · trace the intrusion · escalate

    How this lab works

    1. For every alert, set a Verdict and a Priority from the dropdowns, based on its evidence.
    2. Verdict: False positive (not real), Benign (real but no threat — e.g. an attack that was blocked), Suspicious (needs a look), Malicious (confirmed bad).
    3. Priority: P1 (critical, act now) → P4 (informational). A blocked attack is low priority; an active compromise with data loss is P1.
    4. Submit your triage. You can't move on until you've correctly flagged the alerts that make up the real intrusion — that's the core skill.
    5. Then recover the facts from those alerts to build the flag, and make one escalation decision. Submit the flag on your course platform.
